How to Play

Using the interface

  1. Enter a username. One player hosts and shares the room code; everyone else joins with that code.
  2. The host taps Deal Cards after everyone is in the lobby.
  3. During setup, tap one card in your hand and then one face-up card to swap them. Tap Ready when finished.
  4. On your turn, tap one card or multiple cards of the same rank, then tap Play.
  5. If you cannot or do not want to play, tap Pick Up.
  6. When your hand is empty and the draw pile is gone, play face-up cards. Then tap face-down cards blindly.

Rules

Normal play is the same rank or higher. A 2 resets the count. A 4 is transparent. A 7 makes the next player play 7 or lower. An 8 skips. A 10 clears the pile and cannot be played on a 7. A Joker reverses direction. Four matching ranks in a row clears the pile; transparent 4s do not interrupt the sequence.

While the draw pile remains, draw back up to 3 cards after your turn. The last player still holding cards is the Shithead.

Multiplayer Leaderboard

A win is awarded to the first player to get rid of all cards in a multiplayer game.
