How to Play Shithead

Using the app

  1. Choose Single Player or Multiplayer.
  2. In multiplayer, type a username. One person hosts and shares the room code; everyone else joins with that code.
  3. Each player starts with 3 face-down cards, 3 face-up cards, and 3 cards in hand.
  4. Before play begins, tap a card in your hand and then a face-up card to swap them. Put your strongest cards face-up if you can.
  5. Tap Ready when you are done swapping.
  6. On your turn, tap one card to select it. Tap multiple cards of the same rank to play them together, then tap Play.
  7. If you cannot or do not want to play, tap Pick Up Pile.
  8. When your hand is gone and the draw deck is empty, play your face-up cards. After those are gone, tap a face-down card blindly.

Game rules

Play normally counts upward. If a 6 is on top, the next player may play 6 or higher. Matching ranks may be played together.

  • 2: Magic. Can be played on anything. Resets the count.
  • 4: Magic and transparent. It does not change the active rank or restriction underneath it.
  • 7: Special. Must itself be played legally in numerical order. The next player must then play 7 or lower. After that turn, play returns to normal.
  • 8: Special. Must itself be played legally in numerical order. Skips the next player. Two 8s skip two players, etc.
  • 10: Magic. Clears the pile and the same player goes again. A 10 cannot be played on a 7.
  • Joker: Magic. Reverses direction. Two Jokers reverse twice, leaving direction unchanged.
  • Four in a row: Four of the same rank in succession clears the pile. Transparent 4s do not interrupt the sequence.

If you cannot play, you pick up the entire pile. The next player begins a new pile. While the draw deck has cards, you draw back up to 3 after your turn.

Players who use all of their cards are out. Play continues until only one player still has cards. That player is the Shithead.
