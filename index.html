How to Play

Interface: Choose a mode. In multiplayer, enter a username, then host or join with a room code. During setup, tap one card in your hand and one face-up card to swap them, then tap Ready. During play, select one or more matching cards and tap Play. Tap Pick Up Pile if needed.

Rules: Play the same rank or higher. 2 resets. 4 is transparent. 7 makes the next player play 7 or lower. 8 skips. 10 clears and cannot go on a 7. Joker reverses. Four of the same rank in a row clears the pile, with 4s remaining transparent. Draw back to 3 while the deck remains. After your hand is gone, play face-up cards, then blind face-down cards. Last player holding cards is the Shithead.
