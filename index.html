How to Play Shithead

Each player starts with 3 face-down cards, 3 face-up cards, and 3 cards in hand. Before play starts, you may swap cards between your hand and face-up cards. The goal is to get rid of all your cards. The last player with cards is the Shithead.

Basic play

Play a card of the same rank or higher than the current effective card. You may play multiple cards of the same rank together. If you cannot play, pick up the pile.

Special cards

  • 2: resets the count.
  • 4: transparent and replays the effect of the card underneath it.
  • 7: the next player must play 7 or lower.
  • 8: skips one other player per 8 played.
  • 10: clears the pile and you play again. It cannot be played on a 7.
  • Joker: reverses direction and is transparent for the numerical requirement.

Four of a kind

Four of any rank in a row clears the pile and the player who completes it plays again. Transparent 4s do not interrupt a run of another rank.

Drawing and endgame

While the draw pile remains, draw back up to 3 cards after your turn. Once the draw pile is gone and your hand is empty, play your face-up cards. After those are gone, blind-flip your face-down cards one at a time.

Walkthrough mode

Walkthrough can be toggled on to highlight cards that are legal to play. Face-down cards are never revealed by the walkthrough.
