How to Play Shithead

Each player starts with 3 face-down cards, 3 face-up cards, and 3 cards in hand. Before the game begins, you may swap cards between your hand and your face-up cards so you can save useful cards for later.

The goal

Get rid of every card you have. Players leave the game as they finish, and play continues until only one person is left. Try not to be the Shithead: the last player still holding cards.

Basic play

On a normal turn, you build upward: play a card that is the same rank as or higher than the effective card on top of the pile. For example, on a 6 you may normally play a 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, J, Q, K, or A. You may play multiple cards of the same rank together. If you have no legal play, you must pick up the pile.

Card rules

  • 3, 5, 6, 9, J, Q, K, A: normal cards. They follow the usual same-rank-or-higher rule.
  • 2: resets the pile. It can be played as a magic card and the next player may start again from any rank.
  • 4: transparent. Ignore the 4 when deciding what must be played next; the card underneath it still controls play. If a 4 is played on a special card, it repeats that underlying effect.
  • 7: changes the direction of the count for the next play: the next player must play 7 or lower instead of counting upward. A 10 cannot be played on a 7.
  • 8: skips the next player. Playing multiple 8s skips one player for each 8.
  • 10: clears the entire pile. The player who clears it gets another turn.
  • Joker: reverses the direction of play. Like a 4, it is transparent when determining the numerical requirement for the next card.

Four of a kind

If four cards of the same rank appear consecutively in the pile, the pile is cleared and the player who completes the four-of-a-kind plays again. Transparent 4s do not break a run of another rank.

Your cards

While cards remain in the draw pile, you draw back up to 3 cards in your hand after your turn. Once the draw pile is empty, keep playing from your hand until it is gone. Then play your face-up cards. Once those are gone, you must blind-flip your face-down cards one at a time. If a blind-flipped card cannot legally be played, you pick up the pile.

Winning and losing

Getting rid of your final card takes you out of the game. Everyone else keeps playing. The game ends only when one player remains, and that player is the Shithead.

Walkthrough mode

Turn Walkthrough on if you want the game to highlight cards that are currently legal to play. It will never reveal your face-down cards.
